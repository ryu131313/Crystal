## Reading (2026-10-10)
- **Stable:** 0 isolated errors, 0 FATAL lines, 0 crash files, 0 EncodeOnce warnings, 0 bot errors or disconnects. All 300 bots stayed in game for the hour, and their 300 entries spread across the 4 maps (64/74/74/88). RTT was 1.0 ms. Bots attacked 327k times and killed 28.6k monsters.
- **Memory looks like a plateau, not a leak:**
  - private bytes went 73 → 81 (10 min) → 94 MB (30 min), then 90-92 MB to the end;
  - the 12.6 MB/h slope is dominated by the first half;
  - handles (711 → 719) and threads (27 → 24) are flat.
  - A 2 h soak (the P9 gate) would confirm it.
- **Load:** the game thread was 15-17 % busy at 300 bots on 4 maps, with a 13 ms worst iteration. `obj` and `mob` rose ~20-30 % between the first and last 10 minutes while packets out fell. The likely cause is a change in monster and bot distribution as the maps settled, not a leak; the per-tick costs are well inside budget.
